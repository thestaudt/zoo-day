Stops in order

    Getting there

    Aim to park by 9:30. Weekend lots can fill.

    Directions to Southern Blvd lot Live lot status

    Backup: Fountain Circle via the Fordham Road gate ($30, weekends).

    Star Attractions

    Free with a "Bronx Zoo Admission" ticket, $7 per person each with "Limited Admission." Under 3 is free.

    • On the route: Congo Gorilla Forest and the Wild Asia Monorail
    • Budgie Landing (feed budgies from a seed stick) is extra with every ticket: $5 per person, April to October, go before 3pm

    If you need to cut it short

    • Out of steam after the monorail? Take the Zoo Shuttle from Asia Plaza back to Southern Blvd (Star Attraction) and skip the tigers. Out by about 2:00.
    • Long gorilla line (lines close past a 30-minute wait)? Go on to the giraffes and lions; the monorail covers more big animals.
    • Extra energy: Budgie Landing near Mouse House, $5 per person, feed budgies from a seed stick.

    Not on this route: Himalayan Highlands, Madagascar!, Children's Zoo, World of Birds.

    Boo at the Zoo today

    Free with admission, 11:00 to 4:00 unless noted. Check bronxzoo.com/today for changes.

    • Astor Court (on our walk out, ~2:05): costume parade 12, 1, 2:00, 3:15; dance party with bubbles 15 min after each. Carnival games, lawn games, mural. Face painting ages 3+.
    • Giraffe Corner (we pass ~11:40): Halloween crafts, mystery touch wall. Puppet show 11:30, 12:30, 2:30, 3:30.
    • Grizzly Corner, near the Bears: magic show 11, 12, 2, 3, 4; spooky stories 11:30, 12:30, 2:30, 3:30.
    • Candy Trail, Rhino Trail, 11 to 2. Kids only, one turn per family.
    • Pumpkin Garden (Rhino Garden), carving demos at Dancing Crane (speed carving 11:30, 2:30), hay maze on the Butterfly Patio.
    • Keeper chats: Tigers 10:30, Reptiles 11, Bears 1, Birds of Prey 2.

    If the group splits

    Meet at the Astor Court sea lion pool. Early exits head north to Gate C, 10 to 15 minutes from most stops.
